About us

Serving Alameda County since 1970

TCV Food Bank + Mobile Pantry is the largest client-direct hunger relief organization in Alameda County. We believe access to nutritious food is a right, not a privilege. We've spent over 50 years making that a reality for our neighbors.

We serve all community members regardless of immigration status, income level, or documentation. No referral needed, no questions asked.
